Academic research · NIDS 2026

A Multithreaded Multi-Colony Ant Colony Optimization Framework with Ring and Random Communication Topologies for Solving Large-Scale Sudoku

Presented at the 6th International Conference on Novel & Intelligent Digital Systems (NIDS 2026), University of West Attica, Athens, Greece · September 23–25, 2026

Problem
Existing ant-colony Sudoku solvers run in a single thread, and their success rate drops sharply on large 25×25 puzzles.
Approach
Several cooperating ant colonies run in parallel threads and share solutions through ring and random communication topologies, combined with constraint propagation.
My role
Presenting author, with co-authors Gian Myrl D. Renomeron and John Paul T. Yusiong.
Reported results
On the ten 25×25 test puzzles, the multithreaded solver reached an 83–100% success rate, matching or beating both single-threaded baselines on every puzzle. Full tables are in the slides.
